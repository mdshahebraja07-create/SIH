5. 🤖 AI Career & Course Recommendation

A useful AI feature:

> “What should I learn next?”



The system considers:

Current skills

Assessment performance

Completed courses

Career goal

Interests

Competency gaps


Then recommends the next learning activity.

Example:

> Recommended Next: SQL Intermediate
Reason: Your Data Analytics competency is strong, but SQL assessment performance is below your target level.



Keep recommendations explainable rather than presenting AI as an unquestionable authority.
